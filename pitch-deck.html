Plotline

Environmental work, proven and paid. Farmers and tree planters already do the work. Plotline turns it into signed, located, independently checked proof on an ordinary phone, so funders, programmes and carbon buyers can pay for it.

Offline PWA app · hash-chained registry · independent verifiers · Nigeria

The problem

The work gets done. The people doing it don't get paid.

The work is invisible

Farmers plant trees, protect their soil and restore land every season. Nothing records that it happened, where, or who did it.

Money moves on evidence

Grants, results-based funds and carbon buyers all pay against proof. Without it the money goes to whoever can afford consultants and surveys.

What can't be tracked can't be funded

Many green ideas stall here. A field survey per plot costs more than a smallholder would ever earn from it. Below ~X ha the maths never works.

The payers exist. The missing piece is cheap proof that belongs to the person who did the work.

The insight

The farmers already carry the instrument.

  • Every smallholder has a phone. GNSS accuracy on an ordinary Android is metres — good enough to walk a farm boundary.
  • Offline first, by design. Plotline works with no network at all: maps cached while you have signal, records signed on-device, everything queued and synced later.
  • Crypto without accounts. The farmer's identity is a key on their phone, not a password in a cloud — no signup barrier, no privacy leak.

What that changes

Measurement stops being a service you buy and becomes an action the farmer takes. The verifier's job moves from collecting evidence to checking it — and a registry with cryptographic chain-of-custody can vouch for it.

Marginal cost of a monitored farm drops towards zero.

Product · today, running

From a walk round the farm to proof someone pays for.

1

Map — farmer walks the boundary; the phone logs GNSS points into a polygon. Works offline, offline maps included.

2

Record — trees planted and still alive, soil practices for the season, crop problems seen. Each record is signed by the phone's key as it is made.

3

Submit — a batch with evidence: farm snapshot, satellite checks for PAN and recent fire, NDVI history. Bundled over any connection.

4

Verify — accredited verifiers review the queue and sign approve/reject decisions with their own keys; a credit score gates what they can sign.

5

Get paid — one verified record backs a funder's results payment, a buyer's traceability file, or later a serialised carbon lot (PL-NG-XXXXXXXX).

The farmer's view is one screen: my farm, my records, my payments. The operator console adds market, orders, reconciliation and verifier management.

What counts as work

Three kinds of work a payer can count.

A tree kept alive for a year

A planting record, then a survival count on the same walked plot. A verifier signs the decision with fire and vegetation evidence beside it.

A hectare under a soil practice

Reduced tillage, residue kept, a cover crop, organic fertiliser. Logged per season on a walked boundary. Self-reported today; field verification is the next step.

A report that warns the neighbours

A located crop-disease or pest report with photo fingerprints. Three matching reports within 1 km raise an outbreak alert.

Every unit is signed on the worker's own phone and stays theirs. They can show it to any funder, not only to us.

How the trust works

A registry you can audit without trusting us.

farmer phone (PWA) ECDSA P-256 keypair · records signed at creation · PIN-locked locally batch = records + boundary + satellite evidence → signed submission │ (file bundle, PWA sync, or plain HTTPS when signal exists) ▼ registry server hash-chained append-only ledger (every event, verifiable, head anchored to-chain) evidence checks: fire overlap, PAN, NDVI trajectory, accreditation gates verifier signatures on every decision · methodology registry (MS-1, …) ▼ public views market data, serial lookup, retirement certificates — anyone can verify the chain

Ledger is ours and append-only; the head is anchored externally (Solana) so nobody, including us, can rewrite history unilaterally. Farmer records stay private; only derived, aggregated claims enter the public registry.

Moat

Boring, verified, offline-grade engineering is the moat.

  • Offline-first is hard. Conflict-free merge, signed sync bundles, cached map tiles, install-as-you-update PWA delivery — built and tested (119 automated tests).
  • The chain format — evidence-to-decision lineage with accreditation expiry rules — is the thing registries and buyers will ask for, and we designed to it from day one.
  • Field data compounds. Every farm mapped becomes a permanent, keyed asset; validation history accrues to the farmer and the aggregator.

Not the moat

Another satellite feed. Another Web3 token scheme. Those are commodities; the distribution and the verified-records format are not.

Business model

Payers pay per verified unit of work. The people doing the work never pay.

  • Verification fee — ₦X per verified unit, paid by the funder or programme and shared with the verifier who checked it.
  • Verified-farm fee — ₦X per farm per year from partners who need the records.
  • Carbon share, later — a share of each credit sale once a registered methodology is in place (10% in the current demo split).

The honest carbon number

A farmer keeping 100 trees alive earns about ₦11,700 a year from carbon on our demo assumptions: 18 kg CO₂e per tree-year, ₦12,000 per tonne, a 10% buffer, 60% to the farmer.

Real money, but small and slow. So it sits on top of payments that arrive sooner. These factors are placeholders, not a registered methodology.

Where we are

Working product, not a mock.

Ships today on mid-range Android.

Roadmap

From a first payer to registry-grade issuance.

  • Now — app, verifier queue and registry live: trees, soil practice logs and crop reports, with rainfall, vegetation and fire evidence per plot; pilot set N farms, Plateau.
  • 0–12 months — first payer: a funder or programme paying per verified unit of work for 50–200 farmers and tree planters; payouts through a licensed mobile-money partner.
  • 12–24 months — field verification of soil practices; verified records open input credit and rainfall-index insurance with lender / insurer.
  • 24 months on — carbon: farms pooled under a partner's registered methodology, an accredited verifier, first buyer retirement; ledger head anchored externally.
