Settings

Security, backups and the tools that shape the app on this phone.

Security

A PIN lock is a device-local privacy screen: nothing renders until the PIN unlocks it — every time the app reopens. It is not an account password, and removing it after forgetting only ends in an app reset.

Backup & sync

Your signing key lives on this phone. Back it up so clearing the browser or losing the phone does not cut you off.

On the new device: restore the key first, then import the sync bundle.

Ledger

Optional API key stored on this phone and sent as the Bearer token when anchoring batch fingerprints on the server.

Data
